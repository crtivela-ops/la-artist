About ArtCard.LA

ArtCard.LA is the relaunch of the ArtCard archive, formerly LA-Artist.com. The project began in October 2009 and published cards through 2015. This site brings that collection back online under the name printed on the cards themselves, so it can be browsed, searched, and mapped again.

LA-Artist.com was started in October 2009 by Oliver I. Shipley and Kristen Sheline, both educators in the Los Angeles Unified School District. From 2009 to 2015 the project engaged hundreds of artists in a mail art project called ArtCard.

ArtCards are blank pre-addressed postcards that were distributed across Los Angeles County (in coffee shops, art galleries, community centers, schools, etc.) by volunteers and mailed via the United States Postal Service.

People were encouraged to use each ArtCard as a free 4” × 6” canvas on which to create and mail in their own works of art to be published — first at LA-Artist.com, and now here at ArtCard.LA. The resulting collection of sketches, collages, photographs, and words helps to document L.A. County’s varied creative framework and influence.

Completed ArtCards are archived and mapped online by artist name, L.A. neighborhood and ZIP code. The collection has also been exhibited in museums and galleries, printed in ArtCard Volume 1 and Volume 2, made into pins and stickers, and shared by people online.

The rebrand and relaunch

In November 2026 the archive returned as ArtCard.LA. The new name is the one people already used for the cards, and it marks a new home for the same collection — 1372 ArtCards published on LA-Artist.com between October 29, 2009 and March 2, 2015.

You will still see LA-Artist.com on the original cards, in the books, and in earlier exhibitions. ArtCard.LA is where the archive lives now. You can also follow the archive on Instagram at @artcard.la.

About this archive

ArtCard.LA is a static archive of those published ArtCards. Each card is listed under the artist name and ZIP code written on it, as recorded on the original site. Cards without a usable ZIP code are grouped under Outside LA / California, U.S. States, International and Unknown. Images are shown at the size they were originally published.

All artworks remain © their artists.
