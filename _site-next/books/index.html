ArtCard books

Two books collected the ArtCards in print. Both were published in 2014 and are still available on MagCloud, as a print edition for $35 and as a free digital edition.

ArtCard Volume 1

Published July 24, 2014 · 8″ × 8″ · 48 pages · perfect-bound

Featuring 100+ artists’ work mailed from every corner of Los Angeles, CA.

ArtCard Volume 2

Published June 11, 2014 · 8.25″ × 5.25″ · 94 pages · perfect-bound

The second book in a series of publications featuring art from LA’s largest and most inclusive mail art project. The book spotlights 40 artists who mailed ArtCards in 2013.
